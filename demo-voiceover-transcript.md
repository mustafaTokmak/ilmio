to this exact version of the gift."

"New memory, new brief. The old proposal is superseded, approvals reset to zero,

**1:07 – 1:18** · *Re-rank → calm medallion wins → 3/3 approve*
"The friends rank again, and the calm design wins — one medallion, thin border,
all that air. That's Sam's memory in the design. Three of three approve."

**1:18 – 1:30** · *Story card + AI narrative*
"And Maya doesn't just get a pillow — she gets the story: every memory,
woven by AI into the card that ships with it.
Everyone adds a memory. Together, you make one gift. That's ilmio."
